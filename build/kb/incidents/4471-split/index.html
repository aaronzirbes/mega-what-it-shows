The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. A day-first tenant’s dates come out with day and month swapped (“Booked for 2 December. The invite says 12 February.”). Every tenant on file writes the day first, so every tenant has it, through the app, email and the booking-v2 form. This is the bug the queued fix is for.

hint: the booking-v3 form tells day-first people to type month first. Its date field prints MM/DD/YYYY, but the form reads the value the tenant’s way, day first. People do what the hint says and get a swapped date. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants have it. The parser fix does not touch the form, so these keep coming after it ships. Fix: print the tenant’s own order under the field.

shift: the date moves by one day, not by months. “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.” “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” No day/month swap gives these dates. The pattern fits a date stored or sent as midnight and then shown in another time zone. The desk saw these (desk notes) but filed them under parser by tenant, so they would close with the wrong fix.

Why one ticket hid three

  • Merged on subject. Decision 0001 merges reports when their subject lines match. “Wrong day on the invite” fits all three bugs, so all three landed in one ticket.
  • Intake tags guess from the browser. The “intake tagged as” column shows that intake’s hint tag and the real cause disagree in both directions. The cause depends on the tenant’s locale and booking form (causes.ts), not on the reporter’s browser.
  • The export repeats reports. Its pages overlap, so a raw row count is too high. The table counts each report id once.

What to do

  1. Keep #4471 for parser only and close it when the parser fix ships.
  2. Work the hint and shift tickets apart from it: the parser fix does not close them.
  3. Replace decision 0001 with a new record: merge on cause (tenant and way in), not on subject.
